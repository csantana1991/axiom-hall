The midpoint is the cleanest place to see front-loading, and it uses a double-angle identity you can say in words. The hyperbolic sine of two u equals two times the hyperbolic sine of u times the hyperbolic cosine of u. Set u equal to kappa times T over two, so two u is kappa T. Remaining shares at the midpoint equal X times the hyperbolic sine of kappa T over two, divided by that product. One hyperbolic sine cancels, and you are left with X divided by two times the hyperbolic cosine of kappa T over two. The hyperbolic cosine of zero is one, and for any positive argument the hyperbolic cosine is strictly greater than one.
