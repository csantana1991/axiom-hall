One last distinction keeps the pin from leaking into the ring. The capstone ring moves a market event. It does not score a model. Reproducibility of the ring is a question about the program that moves the event, and this block does not redefine it as a model card. Reproducibility of a model is the card. If you have both, you have two pins, and they point at different objects. Overloading them is how a fast handoff gets described as a reproducible model it does not contain. Say the architecture you meant. If the net is off the path, the hot budget is the handoff, and the card is cold. If the net is on the path, the net eats the budget, and the card is how you name the net that ate it. Either sentence can be respectable. A sentence that claims both the nanosecond path and an unpinned model is not.
