Write the softmax in words, including the stabilization you should actually compute. Let m be the maximum of the scores you are willing to include. Softmax of z at i is e to the power of z sub i minus m, divided by the sum over j of e to the power of z sub j minus m. Subtracting m does not change the softmax. Every term in the numerator and the denominator is multiplied by the same factor, e to the power of negative m, and that factor cancels. What the subtraction changes is the numerics. The largest exponent you actually compute is zero, so you do not overflow. If your paper row disagrees with the lab, the mask or the max-subtraction is the usual culprit. A disagreement that is only "I forgot to subtract the max" should not change the weights once you repair it. A disagreement that is "I included a future index" is not the same bug.
