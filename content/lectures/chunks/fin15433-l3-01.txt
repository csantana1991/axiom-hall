The idea in this block is the sinh schedule. With linear permanent and temporary impact, the mean-variance trajectory for remaining shares is proportional to the hyperbolic sine of kappa times remaining time, divided by the hyperbolic sine of kappa times the horizon. In words, x of t equals X times the hyperbolic sine of kappa times open time, divided by the hyperbolic sine of kappa times T. Kappa grows with risk aversion and with volatility, and it shrinks with temporary impact. As kappa approaches zero the schedule becomes linear. As kappa grows you sell earlier. The lab uses this closed form. It does not run a numerical optimal control on each click.

Check the endpoints before any approximation. At t equals zero, open time equals T, and the hyperbolic sine of kappa T divided by itself is one, provided kappa T is not zero.
